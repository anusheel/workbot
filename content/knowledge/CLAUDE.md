# Knowledge base

One document per file, in a section folder: `policies` (the rules), `manuals`
(how operations and finance work), `it` (how-tos), `faq` (what customers ask)
and `people` (HR). A document's path is `section/slug`.

Front matter has exactly `title`, `owner` (a team, not a person) and `updated`
(ISO date). The first paragraph states the point; `##` headings group the
details and are what search returns, so each heading should stand on its own.
Numbers are numerals with units and currency; limits and deadlines are
specific, and agree with the policies. Write for the person who has to act,
in plain sentences.
