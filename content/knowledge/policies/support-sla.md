Every ticket gets a first human response within the time for its priority,
measured in business hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern).

| Priority | First response  | Update cadence | Resolution target |
| -------- | --------------- | -------------- | ----------------- |
| Urgent   | 1 hour          | Every 4 hours  | 1 business day    |
| High     | 4 hours         | Daily          | 3 business days   |
| Normal   | 1 business day  | Every 3 days   | 5 business days   |
| Low      | 2 business days | Weekly         | 10 business days  |

## Priorities

- Urgent: an order for a customer's live job is wrong or late, or a customer cannot pay or log in
- High: a shipment is delayed, a wrong item was received, or an invoice is disputed
- Normal: questions about products, accounts and deliveries
- Low: feedback and feature requests

## Escalation

A ticket that misses its first-response time is escalated to the support lead
automatically. A ticket open past its resolution target is reviewed in the
daily stand-up until it closes.

## Channels

Email and the web form create tickets. Phone calls are logged as tickets by
the agent who took the call, before the call ends.
