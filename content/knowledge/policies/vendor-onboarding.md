A new vendor is set up before the first order, never after the first invoice.
Operations owns the request; finance owns the approval and the record.

## Documents a vendor provides

- A completed W-9 (or W-8BEN for vendors outside the United States)
- A certificate of insurance when the vendor works on our premises
- Bank details on the vendor's letterhead, confirmed by a phone call to a number from their website, not from the email

## Quotes and approval

| Annual spend        | Quotes needed | Approver                  |
| ------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------- |
| Under $10,000       | 1             | Department head           |
| $10,000 to $100,000 | 3             | Finance lead              |
| Over $100,000       | 3 and a RFP   | CFO and the department VP |

## Payment terms

Standard terms are net 30 from the invoice date. Net 15 is allowed for vendors
under $10,000 a year; anything shorter, or any prepayment, needs the finance
lead's approval. Early-payment discounts of 2% or more are taken.

## Changes to a vendor record

A change of bank details is confirmed by phone with a known contact before it
is saved, and takes effect only after a second finance team member reviews
it. No exceptions, including for urgent requests.
