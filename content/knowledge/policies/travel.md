updated: 2026-08-12

Book travel through the travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Trips
booked later than that need a reason on the request, and your manager's
approval if the fare is more than 20% above the 14-day price.

## Flights

- Economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed above that
- The lowest logical fare within 2 hours of the requested time
- Loyalty points earned on company travel are yours to keep

## Hotels

Nightly caps before tax: $250 in New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich;
$180 in other major cities; $140 elsewhere. Stays over the cap need the
reason on the request. Book cancellable rates whenever the price difference
is under $20.

## Per diem

Travellers may claim the daily meal allowance from the expense policy or
submit receipts, not both. Incidentals such as laundry are covered on trips
of 5 nights or more.

## Changes and cancellations

Tell the travel desk as soon as plans change. Unused non-refundable tickets
are kept as credits on the company account; the desk applies them to the
next booking.
