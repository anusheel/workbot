Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Support agents
resolve refunds themselves within the limits below; anything outside them
goes to the support lead.

## What agents can approve

- Refunds up to $200 per order, to the original payment method
- Store credit up to $300 per order
- Free return shipping when the item arrived damaged or was not what was ordered

## Needs the support lead

- Refunds over $200, or a second refund on the same order
- Returns after 30 days
- Any refund on custom-cut or made-to-order items, which are otherwise final sale

## Damaged and missing items

Ask for a photo of damaged goods; file the carrier claim the same day. Refund
or replace at the customer's choice without waiting for the claim. Missing
items are replaced after the warehouse confirms the pick list.

## Timing

Refunds reach the customer within 5 to 7 business days of approval. Tell the
customer the date, not the range, where the payment provider gives one.
