Every delivery is checked against its purchase order before it leaves the dock. Nothing goes to a shelf unchecked.

## At the dock

- Match the carrier's paperwork to an open purchase order in the system; refuse a delivery with no order and call the buyer
- Count pallets and cartons before signing; note shortages and damage on the carrier's copy and on ours
- Photograph damaged packaging before it is opened

## Checking the contents

- Open 1 carton in 10 for a standard supplier, every carton for a new supplier's first 3 deliveries
- Compare item codes and quantities to the order; book in what arrived, not what was ordered
- Quarantine anything damaged or wrong in bay Q1 with a red tag and the order number

## Closing the receipt

Book the receipt in the system within 2 hours of arrival so finance can match the invoice. A receipt booked late holds up the vendor's payment.
