---
title: Warehouse closing checklist
owner: Operations
updated: 2026-03-30
---

The closing supervisor runs this list after the last pickup and signs it off before arming the alarm.

## Orders

- Every order in the day's waves packed and collected, or carried to tomorrow with a reason
- Returns received today scanned in and placed in the returns bay

## Equipment and stock

- Forklifts on charge, keys in the cabinet
- Quarantine and returns bays tidy and labelled
- Hazardous goods cage locked

## Building

- Dock doors down and locked, lights off except the night lights
- Alarm armed; the last person out notes the time in the log
