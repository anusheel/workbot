Visitors sign in at reception and are escorted while in the building.

## Before the visit

The host books the visitor in the system at least a day ahead with name, company and purpose, so reception has a badge ready.

## On arrival

- The visitor signs in on the tablet and reads the safety notice
- Reception prints a visitor badge; it says VISITOR in red and expires at 6pm
- The host collects the visitor from reception; visitors do not walk unescorted

## On the warehouse floor

Visitors wear a high-visibility vest and stay in the marked walkways. No visitor rides on or operates equipment.

## Leaving

Visitors return the badge and sign out. Reception removes a badge still active at 6pm and tells the host.
