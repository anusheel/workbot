Orders are picked in waves released at 8am, 11am and 2pm. A wave is complete when every order in it is packed and scanned to a lane.

## Picking

- Follow the pick path on the scanner; it is ordered by aisle to keep walking short
- Scan each item's barcode; the scanner rejects a wrong item, do not override it
- Short pick: mark the line short on the scanner and keep going; the stock team resolves shorts within the hour

## Packing

- Fragile items (glass, ceramics, electronics) go in a double-wall carton with 2 inches of padding on every side
- Heavy items (over 20 kg) ship on their own; never on top of lighter goods
- Put the packing slip inside, the label outside, and scan the package to its lane

## Quality

Supervisors audit 20 packed orders a day. More than 1 error in 100 orders for a picker triggers retraining the same week.
