A return arrives with a return number from support. Returns without a number go to bay R0 while support finds the order.

## Inspection

- Scan the return number; the system shows what was authorised and why
- Check the item against the reason: unopened, used, damaged or wrong item
- Grade it: A (resalable as new), B (resalable as open box, 20% off), C (write off)

## Restocking and credit

- Grade A and B go back to stock the same day, B with the open-box sticker
- Credit is released to the customer when the return is graded, not when it arrives; support sees the grade in the order
- Grade C items are photographed and written off at the end of the day

## Timing

Returns are inspected within 1 business day of arrival. The customer's refund then follows the refunds policy: 5 to 7 business days to reach them.
