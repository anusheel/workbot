Stock accuracy is kept by counting a little every day rather than everything once a year.

## Cycle counts

- 50 locations are counted each morning before the first wave, chosen by the system: fast movers weekly, slow movers quarterly
- Count blind: the scanner does not show the expected quantity until the count is entered
- A difference of more than 2 units or 2% is recounted by a second person before the system is adjusted

## Adjustments

Adjustments over $500 in value need the operations lead's approval. Every adjustment carries a reason code: damage, mis-pick, receiving error, theft suspected, unknown.

## Year end

A full wall-to-wall count takes place on the last Saturday of the financial year; shipping stops for the day.
