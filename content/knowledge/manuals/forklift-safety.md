Only certified operators drive forklifts. Certification is renewed every 3 years, and after any incident.

## Every shift

- Inspect the truck before use: brakes, horn, forks, mast and the battery or gas level; log the inspection
- Report a fault and tag the truck out of service; do not drive a tagged truck

## Operating

- 5 mph on the floor, walking pace in the aisles, horn at every aisle end
- Forks low when travelling, loads tilted back, never a person on the forks
- Nobody walks under a raised load; pedestrians have right of way at crossings

## Incidents

Stop, make the area safe, and report to the shift supervisor at once. Near misses are reported too; they are how the next incident is prevented.
