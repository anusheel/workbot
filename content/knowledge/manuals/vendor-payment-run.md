Vendors are paid every Thursday. An invoice is in the run when it is approved and its terms are due by the following Thursday.

## Before the run

- Match every invoice to a purchase order and a booked receipt; unmatched invoices go back to the buyer
- Check bank details against the vendor record; a change of details follows the vendor onboarding policy
- Take early-payment discounts of 2% or more when cash allows

## The run

- The finance lead reviews the proposed run on Wednesday afternoon
- Payments go out Thursday morning by ACH; wires only for international vendors
- Remittance advice is emailed to the vendor automatically

## After

Invoices paid are marked in the system the same day. A vendor asking about a payment gets the date and the reference, which support can see on the vendor record.
