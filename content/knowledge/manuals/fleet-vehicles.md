Fieldstone Supply runs 6 vans for local deliveries and collections. Only drivers on the approved list drive them.

## Booking a van

Book in the fleet calendar with the purpose and the return time. A van not back by its time is chased by the operations desk.

## Before driving

- Walk around the van and note any damage in the log; photograph new damage
- Check fuel, tyres and lights; a van below a quarter tank is refuelled on the company card first

## Incidents

Report any collision, however small, to the operations desk the same day and complete the incident form within 24 hours. Fines incurred while driving are the driver's responsibility, as in the expense policy.

## Servicing

Vans are serviced every 10,000 miles or 6 months. The fleet coordinator books servicing and takes the van off the calendar.
