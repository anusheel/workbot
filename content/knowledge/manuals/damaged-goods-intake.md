Damaged goods come from 2 directions: deliveries from suppliers and returns from customers. Both are recorded the same day.

## From suppliers

- Photograph the pallet or carton before unpacking
- Record the damage against the purchase order line, with the count
- File the carrier claim within 24 hours; the buyer raises a credit request with the supplier

## From customers

- Support files the carrier claim when the customer reports the damage, using the customer's photos
- The customer gets a refund or a replacement at their choice without waiting for the claim, under the refunds policy
- When the item comes back, grade it C unless it is clearly resalable

## Reporting

The weekly operations review sees damage by supplier and by carrier. 3 damaged deliveries from one supplier in a quarter triggers a supplier review.
