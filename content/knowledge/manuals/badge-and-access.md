Every employee and contractor has a badge. The badge opens the doors the role needs and nothing else.

## Getting a badge

- New starters get a badge on day 1 from reception, with a photo taken on the spot
- Contractors get a badge valid for the length of their contract, up to 6 months

## Access levels

- Office: all staff
- Warehouse floor: operations staff and anyone who has done the floor safety briefing
- Server room and finance cabinet: named people only, approved by IT or the finance lead

## Lost badges

Report a lost badge to reception within 1 hour; it is cancelled at once and a new one issued. A second loss in a year is reported to the person's manager.

## The badge printer

Reception's Zebra printer uses 4x6 label stock; a spare roll is kept in the reception cabinet.
