An invoice is approved by the person who ordered the goods or service, within the limits of the vendor onboarding policy.

## Who approves

| Amount            | Approver            |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Under $1,000      | The buyer           |
| $1,000 to $10,000 | The department head |
| Over $10,000      | The finance lead    |

## What an approver checks

- The goods or service arrived, and the receipt is booked
- The price matches the order or the quote
- The invoice is addressed to Fieldstone Supply and shows the purchase order number

## Timing

Approve within 3 business days of the invoice arriving. Invoices waiting longer than 5 days are listed in the Monday finance report, by approver.
