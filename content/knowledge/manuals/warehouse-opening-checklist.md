owner: Operations
updated: 2026-03-30

The opening supervisor runs this list every morning from 6:30am and signs it off in the system by 7am.

## Building

- Disarm the alarm, walk the perimeter doors, check for signs of entry
- Lights, heating or cooling, and the dock doors tested
- Walkways clear, spills cleaned, first-aid kits in place

## Equipment

- Forklifts and pallet jacks inspected and logged by their first operators
- Scanners charged and signed out; printers loaded with labels

## Systems

- The wave for 8am released, shorts from yesterday reviewed
- Carrier pickups for the day confirmed on the dock schedule

A failed check is fixed before the first wave or escalated to the operations lead.
