Every purchase has a purchase order before the money is committed. The order is how receiving, invoicing and payment find each other.

## Raising an order

- Pick the vendor from the vendor list; a new vendor goes through onboarding first
- Enter items, quantities, prices and the delivery date agreed with the vendor
- Approvals follow the amounts in the invoice approval manual

## Changes

A change to quantity or price before delivery is a new version of the order, approved like the original. After delivery, the difference is handled on the invoice.

## Standing orders

Recurring supplies (packaging, labels, cleaning) run on standing orders renewed quarterly. The operations lead reviews quantities against use before each renewal.
