Orders confirmed by 2pm Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day.

## Carriers

- Parcels under 30 kg: UPS Ground by default; UPS 2nd Day Air when the customer paid for express
- Pallets: our regional LTL carrier for addresses within 500 miles, a national carrier beyond
- Hazardous goods (adhesives, aerosols): ground only, with the hazmat label and a trained packer

## Service levels

| Service     | Transit time         | Coverage                 |
| ----------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Ground      | 1 to 5 business days | United States and Canada |
| 2nd Day Air | 2 business days      | United States            |
| LTL freight | 2 to 7 business days | United States and Canada |

## Exceptions

Saturday delivery is available for 2nd Day Air at $18 extra, requested by 12pm on Friday. We do not ship to PO boxes.
