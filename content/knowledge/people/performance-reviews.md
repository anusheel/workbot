Reviews happen twice a year, in January and July. They look back at goals and forward at the next 6 months.

## Before the review

- You write a short self-review against your goals: what went well, what did not, what you want next
- Your manager collects feedback from 2 or 3 people you worked with

## The review

A 45-minute conversation. The outcome is a rating on a 4-point scale and new goals. Pay changes are decided in the January cycle.

## Disagreement

If you disagree with a rating, say so in the review and in writing within 5 business days; the manager's manager and HR review it within 2 weeks.
