Welcome. Your first week is set up so that by Friday you have your tools, know your team and have done one piece of real work.

## Day 1

- Reception gives you your badge and laptop; IT's setup takes about 20 minutes
- Your manager walks you through the team, the shared drive and the requests queue
- Office staff do the floor safety briefing before any visit to the warehouse

## Days 2 to 4

- Shadow 2 colleagues on their usual work
- Read the policies for your team in the knowledge base; ask WorkBot when something is unclear
- Set up your email signature, phone greeting and time-off calendar

## Day 5

You and your manager agree your first 30-day goals and write them in the people system.
