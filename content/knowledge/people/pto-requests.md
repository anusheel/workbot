Full-time employees have 20 days of paid time off a year, plus public holidays. Days accrue monthly and up to 5 unused days carry into the next year.

## Requesting

- Request in the people system at least 2 weeks ahead for 3 days or more, 3 business days ahead for shorter breaks
- Your manager approves within 3 business days; the calendar shows approved time off to the team

## Busy periods

Operations and support plan around the year-end count and the holiday season; requests in those weeks are approved first come, first served, up to 2 people per team.

## Changes

Cancel or move approved time off in the system; your manager is told automatically.
