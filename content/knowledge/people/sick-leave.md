If you are ill, stay home. Tell your manager before your shift starts, by message or phone.

## How it works

- Up to 10 paid sick days a year; no note needed for 1 or 2 days
- From the 3rd consecutive day, a doctor's note
- Longer illness moves to the company's disability cover from week 3; HR explains the steps

## Caring for family

Sick days can be used to care for a sick child or partner. Say so when you tell your manager.

## Coming back

Warehouse staff returning after more than 5 days have a short check-in with their supervisor before going back on equipment.
