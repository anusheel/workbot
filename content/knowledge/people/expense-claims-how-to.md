Expenses are claimed in the expense tool, and paid with payroll. The expense reimbursement policy says what is covered; this is how to claim.

## Steps

1. Photograph the receipt the day you get it; the app reads the amount and date
2. Pick the category and, for client meals, add the attendees
3. Submit; your manager sees it the same day

## Receipts

Every claim over $25 needs an itemised receipt. A card slip alone is not a receipt. Lost a receipt? Say so on the claim; your manager decides.

## When you are paid

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run, within 10 business days of approval. Claims older than 30 days need the finance lead's approval, so claim weekly.
