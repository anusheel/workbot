Refer someone for an open role and, if they are hired and stay 6 months, you receive $1,500 with the next payroll.

## How to refer

Submit the candidate's name and resume through the careers page's referral form before they apply. Referrals after the candidate has applied do not qualify.

## Rules

- Managers cannot refer into their own team
- Agency candidates and former employees do not qualify
- One bonus per hire; if 2 people refer the same candidate, the earlier referral counts
