Pallet deliveries are booked with you in advance so someone is there to receive them.

## Booking

The carrier calls or emails to agree a date and a 4-hour window, usually 2 business days before delivery. You can also pick a window from the tracking page.

## On the day

- Count the pallets and cartons before signing; note any shortage or damage on the carrier's paperwork
- Deliveries are kerbside unless you ordered inside delivery

## Missed appointments

If nobody is there, the carrier tries once more at no charge; a third attempt is charged at $75 or the order is returned to us.
