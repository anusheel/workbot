---
title: Can I change my order?
owner: Support
updated: 2026-07-20
---

Orders can be changed until they are picked, which is usually within 2 hours of confirmation on a business day.

## What can change

- Quantities, items and the shipping address, before picking
- The shipping service, before picking
- The billing address and purchase order number, at any time before the invoice

## How

Reply to the order confirmation email or call support with the order number. Support can see whether the order has been picked.

## After picking

Once picked, the order ships as placed. Refuse the delivery or return it under the returns process; shipping both ways is refunded only if we made an error.
