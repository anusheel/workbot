Every order has an invoice, available from the order page and emailed when the order ships.

## Getting a copy

Sign in and open the order; "Download invoice" gives a PDF. Support can email a copy to the address on the account.

## Changing an invoice

Billing address, company name and purchase order number can be corrected before payment is due; ask support with the order number. Prices and quantities cannot change on an invoice; a credit note is issued instead.

## Payment terms

Card orders are charged when the order ships. Account customers pay on net 30 terms from the invoice date, by ACH or card.
