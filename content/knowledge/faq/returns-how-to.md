title: How do I return something?
owner: Support
updated: 2026-06-30
---

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unopened or in resalable condition.

## Steps

1. Contact support with the order number and the reason; you receive a return number and a label within 1 business day
2. Pack the item in its original packaging where possible, with the return number inside
3. Drop it at any UPS location within 14 days of receiving the label

## Refund

The refund is released when the return is inspected, within 1 business day of arriving at our warehouse, and reaches you within 5 to 7 business days. Items returned used or incomplete may be refunded as open box, at 20% less.

## Return shipping

Free when the item arrived damaged or was not what you ordered. Otherwise $9 per parcel, deducted from the refund.
