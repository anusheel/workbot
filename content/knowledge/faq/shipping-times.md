---
title: How long does shipping take?
owner: Support
updated: 2026-09-02
---

Orders confirmed by 2pm Eastern on a business day ship the same day; later orders ship the next business day.

## Transit times

- Ground: 1 to 5 business days across the United States and Canada
- 2nd Day Air: 2 business days in the United States; order by 2pm Eastern
- Pallet freight: 2 to 7 business days, with a delivery appointment

## Tracking

The shipping confirmation email has the tracking link. Tracking usually updates within 24 hours of the order leaving us.

## Delays

If tracking has not moved for 3 business days, contact support with the order number; we open a trace with the carrier the same day.
