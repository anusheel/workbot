Orders of 50 units or more of one item, or over $5,000, are bulk orders with their own pricing and delivery.

## Pricing

Ask support or your account manager for a quote; bulk prices are valid for 30 days. Quotes over $10,000 are approved by the sales lead within 2 business days.

## Delivery

Bulk orders ship on pallets by freight, 2 to 7 business days, with a delivery appointment. Tell us if the address has no dock or forklift; we send a truck with a lift gate at no charge.

## Returns

Bulk orders can be returned within 30 days for unopened cartons only, with a 15% restocking fee unless the error was ours.
