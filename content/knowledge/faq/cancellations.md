---
title: Cancelling an order
owner: Support
updated: 2026-07-20
---

An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is picked. After that it becomes a return.

## Before picking

Contact support with the order number; the cancellation and the refund are confirmed by email within an hour on a business day. The refund reaches you within 5 to 7 business days.

## After picking or shipping

Refuse the delivery, or accept it and return it within 30 days. Return shipping is at your cost unless the item is wrong or damaged.

## Custom-cut and made-to-order items

These cannot be cancelled once production has started, usually the next business day after confirmation.
