We are sorry. Send us photos and we will put it right without waiting for the carrier.

## What to send

- Photos of the damage and of the packaging, including the shipping label
- The order number

## What we do

- You choose a refund or a replacement; either is arranged the same business day
- Return shipping is free; keep the item until support says whether it needs to come back
- We file the carrier claim ourselves

## Refunds

A refund reaches you within 5 to 7 business days of approval. Support agents can approve refunds up to $200 per order on the spot; larger refunds go to the support lead the same day.
