Fieldstone Supply products carry a 1-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship from the date of delivery.

## What is covered

Products that fail under normal use. We repair, replace or refund at our choice.

## What is not covered

Damage from misuse, overloading beyond the rated capacity, modification, or normal wear.

## Making a claim

Contact support with the order number, photos and a description of the fault. We answer within 2 business days and arrange collection where needed at no cost.
