title: How quickly will support answer?
owner: Support
updated: 2026-09-05
---

Every message gets a first reply from a person within the time for its priority, during business hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern).

## Response times

- Urgent (an order for a live job is wrong or late, you cannot pay or sign in): within 1 hour
- High (a delayed shipment, a wrong item, a disputed invoice): within 4 hours
- Normal (questions about products, accounts and deliveries): within 1 business day
- Low (feedback and ideas): within 2 business days

## Channels

Email and the web form create a ticket at once. Phone lines are open in business hours; outside them, leave a message or write to us.
