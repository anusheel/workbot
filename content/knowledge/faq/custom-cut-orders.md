Shelving, panels and matting cut to your sizes are made for you and are final sale.

## Ordering

Give sizes in whole centimetres or inches, and say which. Support confirms the sizes back to you by email before production starts; check them.

## Timing

Production starts the next business day after you confirm the sizes and takes 5 to 10 business days. After that, shipping times apply.

## Changes and returns

Sizes can be changed until production starts. Custom items cannot be returned, except when they arrive damaged or do not match the confirmed sizes, in which case we remake them.
