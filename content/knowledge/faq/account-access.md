owner: Support
updated: 2026-04-02
---

Your account uses your email address and a password, or a sign-in link sent by email.

## Forgotten password

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page; the reset email arrives within a minute. Check the spam folder; the link is valid for 1 hour.

## Changing your email

Sign in and change the email under Account; you confirm from both the old and the new address.

## Several people, one account

A business account can have up to 10 users with their own sign-ins; the account owner adds them under Users. Sharing one sign-in between people is not supported and makes order history unclear.
