We accept credit and debit cards, ACH for account customers, and purchase orders from approved organisations.

## Cards

Visa, Mastercard and American Express. The card is charged when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Account terms

Businesses can apply for an account with net 30 terms after 3 paid orders. Approval takes up to 5 business days and needs a credit reference.

## Purchase orders

Schools, hospitals and public bodies can order against a purchase order; send it to support with the order and we invoice on net 30 terms.

## Declined payments

A declined card holds the order for 48 hours while you try another method; after that the order is cancelled.
