Passwords are reset by you, from the sign-in page, in under 2 minutes. IT does not set passwords for people.

## Resetting

1. On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password"
2. Approve the prompt on your phone, or enter the code from your authenticator
3. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters, not used before

## Locked out

5 wrong attempts lock the account for 15 minutes. If you cannot approve on your phone (lost or new phone), call IT on extension 400; they verify your identity with your manager before clearing the lock.

## Rules

Never share a password or an approval prompt with anyone, including IT. IT will never ask for one.
