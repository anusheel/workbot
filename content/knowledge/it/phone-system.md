Desk phones and the softphone app ring the same extension. Support calls go through the support queue.

## Extensions

Your extension is in the directory and your email signature. Dial 9 for an outside line. The main number is answered by reception from 8am to 6pm Eastern.

## Support queue

Support agents sign in to the queue at the start of their shift. Calls wait up to 3 minutes before overflowing to the next available agent; after 6 minutes the caller is offered a callback.

## Voicemail

Voicemail arrives in email as a recording and a transcript. Set a greeting on your first day and an out-of-office greeting when you are away for more than a day.

## Recording

Support calls are recorded for 90 days under the data retention policy; callers hear a notice.
