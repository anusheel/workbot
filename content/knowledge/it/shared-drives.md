Each team has a shared drive. Company documents live there, not in personal folders or email.

## Layout

- `Operations`, `Finance`, `Support`, `People` and `IT`: one drive per team, owned by the team lead
- `Company`: policies and manuals, read-only for everyone
- `Projects`: one folder per project, owned by its lead and archived 6 months after it closes

## Access

Team members get their team's drive on day 1. Access to another team's drive is requested from that team's lead through the IT portal and reviewed every 6 months.

## Sharing outside

Share a file with a customer or vendor by link with an expiry date, 30 days at most. Never share a whole folder outside the company.
