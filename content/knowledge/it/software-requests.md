New software is requested on the IT portal. IT checks security and licensing before anyone installs it.

## What to include

- What you need to do, and why the tools you have do not do it
- The vendor, the price per person per month, and how many people need it
- Whether it handles customer or employee data

## Approval

- Under $50 per person per month: IT approves within 3 business days
- Above that, or anything handling personal data: IT and the department head, within 10 business days

## Installing

Approved software appears in the self-service portal. Installing software another way is not allowed on company laptops; the security agent reports it.
