Office printers are added automatically on the office wifi. Label printers in the warehouse are managed by operations.

## Printing

Send the job to "Office printer"; collect it by tapping your badge on any office printer within 24 hours. Uncollected jobs are deleted.

## Problems

- Paper jam: follow the printer's screen; do not pull paper against the rollers
- Toner: a spare for each model is in the supplies cupboard; ask office management when the last one is used
- Not printing at all: restart the laptop first; then report it on the IT portal with the printer's name

## Label printers

The Zebra printers at reception and on the pack benches take 4x6 labels. Operations keeps the stock; see the badges manual for the reception printer.
