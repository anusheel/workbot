Everyone uses the same signature, set by the email system. There is nothing to install.

## What it contains

Name, role, team, the main office number and your extension, and the company website. No quotes, images or personal social links.

## Changing your details

Update your role or extension in the directory; the signature follows within an hour. If your name is wrong, ask HR to correct the directory.

## External email

External messages carry a short line saying the message is from outside the company's domain. Treat links in them with care, as the security incident manual says.
