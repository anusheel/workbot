---
title: Reporting a security incident
owner: IT
updated: 2026-09-15
---

Report anything that looks like a security problem within 1 hour, even if you are not sure. A false alarm costs minutes.

## What counts

- A lost or stolen laptop, phone or badge
- An email asking for a password, a payment or a change of bank details
- A link you clicked and regret, or software you did not install appearing on your laptop
- Customer or employee data sent to the wrong person

## How to report

Call IT on extension 400, or use "Report a security incident" on the IT portal out of hours. Do not forward a suspicious email; use the "Report phishing" button instead.

## What happens next

IT contains the problem first (locking accounts, wiping devices), then investigates. Data exposure is handled under the incident response policy, with the customer told within 72 hours where the law requires it.
