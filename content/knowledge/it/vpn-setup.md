The VPN is required on any network that is not your home network or the office, as the remote work policy says.

## Installing

1. Install the VPN client from the self-service portal
2. Sign in with your company email; approve the prompt on your phone
3. Choose the "Fieldstone" profile; it connects automatically on untrusted networks

## If it does not connect

- Check the date and time on the laptop; a clock more than 5 minutes off blocks the connection
- Turn off any other VPN or proxy
- Restart the client; if it still fails, send IT the client log from Help, Export log

## Speed

The VPN routes only company traffic; video calls and streaming do not go through it, so they are not slowed down.
