A new laptop arrives set up with the company image. The first sign-in finishes the setup in about 20 minutes.

## First sign-in

1. Connect to wifi and sign in with your company email
2. Set up your phone as the second factor when asked
3. Let the device finish installing: the chat app, the browser profile, the VPN and the security agent

## What is already there

- Disk encryption, screen lock after 5 minutes, automatic updates
- The printer at your office and the shared drives for your team

## Your old laptop

Hand the old laptop to IT within a week. Copy nothing to personal storage; your files are in the shared drives and your home folder is synced.
