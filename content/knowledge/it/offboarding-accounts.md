title: Offboarding accounts
owner: IT
updated: 2026-05-30
---

When someone leaves, their accounts close on their last day and their devices come back within a week.

## The manager's part

- Tell HR the last day at least 5 business days ahead; HR opens the offboarding checklist
- Name who takes over the person's files, shared drive ownership and open requests

## IT's part

- Accounts disabled at 6pm on the last day; email forwards to the manager for 30 days
- Shared drive ownership moved to the named person
- Laptop, phone and badge collected and wiped; the asset record updated

## Contractors

Contractor accounts expire automatically at the contract end date set when the badge was issued.
