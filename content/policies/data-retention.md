Keep records as long as the law and the business need them, and no longer.
Deletion is automatic where the system supports it and scheduled where it
does not.

## Retention periods

- Financial records, invoices and contracts: 7 years after the tax year ends
- Support tickets and their attachments: 3 years after closing
- Call recordings: 90 days, unless attached to an open dispute
- Application and access logs: 1 year
- Candidate applications: 1 year after the position closes, unless the candidate agrees to longer

## Customer requests

A customer may ask what we hold about them, or ask us to delete it. Support
logs the request as a ticket for IT, and IT answers within 30 days.
Financial records are kept for their full period even after a deletion
request; the customer is told so.

## Backups

Backups follow the same periods. A record deleted from a system is gone from
backups within 35 days.
