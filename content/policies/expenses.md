Fieldstone Supply reimburses reasonable expenses incurred for work. Submit
them in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase; later claims need
the finance lead's approval.

## What is covered

- Meals while travelling: up to $60 per day, including tips
- Ground transport: taxis, rideshare and public transport on work trips; mileage at the IRS rate for a personal car
- Equipment under $500 that your manager approved in writing beforehand
- Software subscriptions under $50 per month, approved by IT
- Client meals: up to $75 per person, with the attendees named on the claim

## What is not covered

- Alcohol, except with clients and within the client meal limit
- Fines, parking tickets and upgrades to travel class
- Commuting between home and the office

## Receipts and approval

Every claim over $25 needs an itemised receipt. Your manager approves claims
up to $1,000; the finance lead approves anything above that. Finance pays
approved claims with the next payroll run, within 10 business days of approval.

## Company cards

Card holders reconcile their statement by the 5th of the following month. An
unreconciled statement suspends the card until it is complete.
