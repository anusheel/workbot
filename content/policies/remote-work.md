Office roles may work from home up to 3 days a week, agreed with their
manager. Warehouse and field roles follow their site schedule.

## Hours

Core hours are 10am to 3pm in your office's time zone. Outside them, keep your
calendar current and respond to messages within half a business day.

## Equipment

A one-time stipend of $400 covers a desk, chair or monitor. IT provides the
laptop and replaces it every 4 years; personal computers are not used for
work.

## Security

- Work only on the company laptop, with the disk encrypted and updates installed within 7 days
- Use the VPN on any network that is not your home network
- No company data on paper outside the office; shred anything printed at home
- Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 1 hour

## Travel while remote

Working from another country for more than 14 days in a year needs approval
from HR for tax and insurance reasons.
