JOB POSTING

Title: Dispatch and Route Coordinator
Company: Fieldstone Supply
Location: Oakland, CA
Pay: $29.50 - $34.00 USD per hour
Workplace: On-site at our Oakland warehouse. This role is not remote.

About the role
Fieldstone Supply is a building-supply company with a warehouse in Oakland and a fleet of 6 delivery vans. We are hiring a Dispatch and Route Coordinator to keep contractor orders moving from the warehouse floor to job sites on time.

Responsibilities
- Plan daily delivery routes for the 6 delivery vans
- Assign drivers and loads each morning based on order priority and van capacity
- Update customers when delivery windows change
- Reconcile signed delivery receipts with the operations team at end of day
- Report damaged or short shipments to the support team

Requirements
- 2+ years of dispatch or logistics experience
- Comfortable using route-planning software and spreadsheets
- Able to lift 40 lbs and walk the warehouse yard
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Valid California driver's license

To apply, email your resume to jobs@fieldstonesupply.example with the subject line Dispatch Coordinator.
