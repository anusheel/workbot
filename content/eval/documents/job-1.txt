Forklift Operator (Day Shift)
Fieldstone Supply · Oakland, CA · On-site · Full-time

Pay: $24.00 - $28.00 per hour, depending on experience

About the role
You will move inbound pallets from the dock to racking, pick orders for the afternoon routes, and keep the receiving bay clear.

Responsibilities
- Unload deliveries and book them in within 2 hours of arrival
- Pick and stage orders for the delivery vans
- Carry out the daily forklift inspection and report faults
- Keep bay Q1 and the quarantine tags in order

Requirements
- Valid forklift certification (counterbalance)
- 1+ year in a warehouse or yard
- Able to lift 50 lb and work on your feet for a shift
