Fieldstone Supply - Job Posting

Position: Dispatch Coordinator
Company: Fieldstone Supply
Location: Oakland, CA
Workplace: On-site at our Oakland warehouse. This position is not remote or hybrid.
Pay: $29.50 to $34.25 per hour, depending on experience.

About the role
Fieldstone Supply is a building-supply company serving contractors across the East Bay. Our operations team is looking for a Dispatch Coordinator to keep our 6 delivery vans moving and our customers informed. Posted 14 March; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

Responsibilities
- Schedule and route daily deliveries across our fleet of 6 vans
- Coordinate with warehouse staff so orders are staged and loaded on time
- Update customers by phone and email when delivery windows change
- Log delivery confirmations and report exceptions to the operations manager

Requirements
- 2+ years of dispatch or logistics experience
- Proficiency with routing software and spreadsheets
- Clear written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to lift 40 lbs occasionally
- Valid California driver's license

To apply, send your resume to hiring@fieldstonesupply.example with the subject line Dispatch Coordinator.
