Fieldstone Supply
Job Opening: Route Dispatch Coordinator
Posted: March 12

Location: Oakland, CA
Workplace: On-site at our Oakland warehouse. This role is not remote or hybrid.
Pay: $29.50 to $34.75 per hour, depending on experience

About the role
Fieldstone Supply is a building-supply company with a warehouse in Oakland and a fleet of 6 delivery vans. We are hiring a Route Dispatch Coordinator to keep contractor orders moving from the loading dock to the job site.

Responsibilities
- Build and assign daily delivery routes for our 6 vans
- Confirm delivery windows with contractors and job-site foremen
- Coordinate with warehouse staff so orders are staged and loaded on time
- Track van locations and update customers when deliveries are delayed
- Reconcile signed delivery receipts with the operations team at end of day

Requirements
- At least 2 years of dispatch or logistics coordination experience
- Proficiency with routing or fleet-tracking software
- Clear written and phone communication skills
- Ability to work an early shift starting at 5:30 AM
- Ability to lift 40 lbs when helping at the loading dock

To apply, send your resume to hiring@fieldstonesupply.example with the subject line Dispatch Coordinator.
